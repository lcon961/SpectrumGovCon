Pricing & strategic growth talent for GovCons

Pricing analysts, estimators, and strategists who build compliant, competitive bids and help your business grow into its next contract vehicle.

What We Support

Win the work, and grow the business

A strong bid depends on a cost volume that is both competitive and defensible. Our pricing professionals build bases of estimate, rate models, and cost volumes that hold up to evaluation and to audit.

Beyond individual bids, we place strategists who connect pricing to growth: rate structures that keep you competitive as you scale, pipeline and market analysis, and financial planning for new vehicles, recompetes, and acquisitions.

Roles we place

  • Pricing analysts & managers
  • Cost estimators
  • Directors of pricing
  • Price-to-win analysts
  • Growth & strategy analysts
  • FP&A and business planning leads
