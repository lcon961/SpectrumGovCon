Pricing analysts, business development leads, capture managers, and strategists who find the right federal, state, and local opportunities and build compliant, competitive bids to win them.

What We Support

Win the work, and grow the business

A strong bid depends on a cost volume that is both competitive and defensible. Our pricing professionals build bases of estimate, rate models, and cost volumes that hold up to evaluation and to audit.

Growth starts well before the RFP. We place business development and capture professionals who build your pipeline across federal, state, and local markets, qualify the right pursuits, and position you with the customer early.

Then we connect that pipeline to pricing and planning: rate structures that keep you competitive as you scale, and financial planning for new vehicles, recompetes, and acquisitions.

Federal, State & Local

Growth across every level of government

Federal and state and local markets buy differently. We place BD and capture professionals who know the rules, portals, and relationships in each.

/ 01

Federal

Civilian, defense, and intelligence community agencies, from first sources sought to award.

  • SAM.gov notices, agency forecasts & industry days
  • GSA Schedules, GWACs, IDIQs & OTAs
  • 8(a), HUBZone, WOSB & SDVOSB set-asides
  • Small business office & OSDBU engagement
  • Prime and subcontractor teaming strategy
/ 02

State & local (SLED)

States, counties, cities, transit and utility authorities, and public education, each with its own rules.

  • State portals like Virginia's eVA and Maryland's eMMA
  • Cooperative contracts such as NASPO ValuePoint & Sourcewell
  • SWaM, MBE, and DBE certifications & participation goals
  • Public RFP timelines, protests & council approvals
  • Local relationships & procurement office outreach

When Clients Call Us

Common situations we staff for

/ 01

A proposal surge

Three RFPs land in the same month and your pricing team is two people. We send experienced pricers who can own a cost volume from kickoff through final production.

/ 02

Defending a recompete

Incumbents lose on price more often than on performance. A price-to-win analyst models the competition and shows you where to land without giving away margin.

/ 03

Forward pricing rates

Building a forward pricing rate proposal, or negotiating an agreement with DCMA, takes rate modeling experience. Our consultants have been on both sides of that table.

/ 04

Growth & acquisitions

Bidding a new vehicle, crossing a size standard, or integrating an acquired company changes your rate structure. We place strategists who model it before it surprises you.
