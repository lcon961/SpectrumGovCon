Contracts administration talent

Contracts and subcontracts professionals who manage your portfolio from pre-award through closeout, fluent in the FAR and DFARS.

What We Support

Every contract managed from award to closeout

Contracts and subcontracts administrators protect your company's interests and keep it compliant. They review solicitations, negotiate terms, manage modifications, and make sure the right FAR and DFARS clauses flow down to your subcontractors.

We place contracts professionals who can step into a busy portfolio — prime contracts, subcontracts, IDIQs, and GSA Schedules — and keep it moving.

Roles we place

  • Contracts administrators
  • Subcontracts administrators
  • Contracts managers & directors
  • Contract closeout specialists
  • GSA Schedule specialists
  • Contracts paralegals
