Industry

Back-office talent for federal logistics

Procurement, accounting, HR, and compliance professionals for contractors who move, store, maintain, and supply for the federal government.

What Makes It Different

High volume, many sites, and a lot of government property

Logistics and supply chain contracts run on purchasing volume, government-furnished property, and large hourly workforces spread across many sites, sometimes overseas. Each of those creates back-office work that has to stand up to review.

We place consultants who know how to run a compliant purchasing system, track and report government property, bill across multiple locations and contract line items, and keep Service Contract Act wages and fringe benefits correct.

What our consultants know

  • Purchasing systems & CPSR readiness
  • Government property management (FAR 52.245-1)
  • Supplier & subcontractor management
  • Multi-site & CLIN-level billing
  • Service Contract Act wages & fringe
  • OCONUS workforce administration
