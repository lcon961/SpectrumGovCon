What Makes It Different

The most demanding customer in government contracting

Defense and aerospace contractors live under the closest oversight in the federal market. Cost-type and incentive contracts, CAS coverage, DCAA and DCMA audits, and DFARS business system rules all land on the back office, and a single deficiency can mean withheld payments.

We send consultants who have worked inside DoD primes and subcontractors, so they already speak the language of incurred cost submissions, EVM reviews, and clause flowdowns on day one.

What our consultants know

  • DCAA audits & incurred cost submissions
  • Cost Accounting Standards (CAS) & disclosure statements
  • Earned value management (EIA-748)
  • DFARS business systems
  • CMMC & NIST SP 800-171 readiness
  • ITAR & export control flowdowns
