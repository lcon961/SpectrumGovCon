Industry

Back-office talent for professional services

Capture, pricing, accounting, and HR professionals for consulting, advisory, engineering, and program support firms that sell expertise to the government.

What Makes It Different

Your people are the product

Professional services firms win on the strength of their key personnel and grow by winning the next recompete. That puts heavy pressure on proposals, pricing, and indirect rates, and on keeping the right people billable.

We place consultants who can support a proposal surge, keep rates competitive as overhead grows, and manage the recruiting and retention that a people-first business depends on.

What our consultants know

  • GSA Multiple Award Schedule & agency IDIQs
  • Key personnel & resume-driven proposals
  • Indirect rate structures & utilization
  • Labor-hour & fixed-price billing
  • Recompete & growth planning
  • Small business programs & set-asides
