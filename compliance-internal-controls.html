Internal controls & compliance talent

Compliance and internal controls specialists who keep your business systems, policies, and processes audit-ready.

What We Support

Be ready before the auditors arrive

Government customers expect contractors to maintain adequate business systems, written policies, and internal controls — and DCAA and DCMA reviews will test them. Gaps can mean withheld payments or lost opportunities.

We place compliance and internal controls professionals who can assess where you stand, document and fix what's missing, and support you through the review itself.

Roles we place

  • Compliance managers
  • Internal auditors
  • Internal controls specialists
  • Policies & procedures writers
  • Audit readiness consultants
  • Ethics & compliance program leads
