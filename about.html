About Us

30 years of GovCon back-office expertise

Spectrum Careers' GovCon Practice provides exceptional back-office solutions for GovCons, both large and small, throughout the United States.

Our Story

Built on three decades in the DMV

Government contracting firms need flexible support that is highly vetted and rapidly available. For thirty years, Spectrum Careers has served the Washington, D.C. metro area, and government contracting has become our deepest practice.

We provide exceptional consultants at a moment's notice, so your team can seamlessly continue doing what it does best, without worrying about talent shortages.

Who we work with

  • Defense & aerospace firms, cleared and non-cleared
  • Technology & IT services providers
  • Healthcare, logistics & professional services GovCons
  • Small and midsize government contractors
