About Us

Contracting expertise, on your side of the table

SpectrumGovCon was founded to give small and mid-size businesses the same caliber of federal contracting support that large primes build in-house.

Our Story

Built by people who've sat in the room

Federal contracting is a specialized discipline — the rules, timelines, and evaluation criteria don't work like the commercial market. SpectrumGovCon exists to close that gap for businesses that don't have a full capture and proposal shop of their own.

We work as an extension of your team: close enough to understand your business, experienced enough to know what agencies and evaluators are actually looking for.

Who we work with

  • Small businesses pursuing their first federal contracts
  • Growing contractors expanding into new agencies or vehicles
  • Teams preparing for a specific pursuit or recompete
  • Businesses navigating certification (8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB, HUBZone)
