Pricing & procurement talent for GovCons

Pricing analysts, estimators, and procurement professionals who build compliant, competitive bids and purchasing processes that stand up to review.

What We Support

Win the work, and buy it compliantly

A strong bid depends on a cost volume that is both competitive and defensible. Our pricing professionals build bases of estimate, rate models, and cost volumes that hold up to evaluation and to audit.

On the procurement side, we place buyers and subcontract specialists who understand flowdowns, supplier cost and price analysis, and what a Contractor Purchasing System Review looks for.

Roles we place

  • Pricing analysts & managers
  • Cost estimators
  • Directors of pricing
  • Buyers & purchasing agents
  • Procurement managers
  • Subcontract cost/price analysts
