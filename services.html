What We Do

GovCon advisory services

Hands-on support across the federal contracting lifecycle — from identifying the right opportunities to staying compliant after award.

01 — Capture & Business Development

Find and pursue the right opportunities

We help you build a realistic, targeted pipeline instead of chasing every RFP. That means understanding your true differentiators, mapping them to agency needs, and building relationships before the opportunity drops.

  • Market research & opportunity identification
  • Agency & customer relationship mapping
  • Competitive landscape & win probability analysis
  • Teaming partner identification & outreach
  • Capture planning & pursuit decision support
  • Proposal management & scheduling
  • Technical, management & past performance volumes
  • Compliance matrices & RFP shredding
  • Color team reviews (Pink, Red, Gold)
  • Pricing strategy & cost volume coordination
  • Desktop publishing & final production
02 — Proposal Development

Compliant proposals that score

We write and manage proposals built around what evaluators actually score — compliance, clarity, and a well-articulated win theme — not just paperwork to check a box.

03 — Contract Vehicles & Compliance

Get on the right vehicles, and stay compliant

Winning a contract vehicle is only the start. We help you get positioned on the vehicles that matter and maintain the compliance posture needed to keep — and grow — that business.

  • GSA Multiple Award Schedule (MAS) support
  • IDIQ & GWAC positioning strategy
  • Small business certifications (8(a), SDVOSB, WOSB, HUBZone)
  • Post-award compliance & audit readiness
  • Contract administration & modification support
