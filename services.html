Where We Help

Outsourced & interim GovCon back-office solutions

CFO and finance leadership, controller and accounting leadership, contracts and subcontracts administration, pricing and growth, and program finance control, delivered fractional, interim, or direct hire.

01 · Fractional CFO

Senior finance leadership, on your terms

Pricing and rate strategy, cash management, audit readiness, and support for growth or exit, without the cost of a full-time hire. Most engagements start with a fifteen-minute conversation and end with a clear recommendation.

  • Pricing & rate strategy
  • Cash flow forecasting & treasury management
  • Budgeting, forecasting & annual operating plans
  • Cost Accounting Standards (CAS)
  • Financial statement preparation & external audit support
  • Corporate tax
  • DCAA compliance
  • Incurred cost submissions
  • Indirect rate structures
  • Month-end close
  • Chart of accounts design & restructure
  • Revenue recognition (ASC 606)
02 · Fractional Controller

Hands-on accounting leadership that's always audit-ready

Month-end close, DCAA-compliant processes, indirect rates, and books that are always audit-ready. It's hands-on accounting leadership built for the realities of government contracting.

03 · Interim Solutions

Vetted consultants who step in fast

Vetted consultants who step in fast to cover departures, surges, and transitions across your entire back office. First candidates typically arrive within one to two business days.

  • Contracts administration
  • Government billing & closeouts
  • Deltek Costpoint & Unanet
  • Outsourced AP / vendor management
  • AR, collections & DSO management
  • Payroll processing & multi-state tax registration
