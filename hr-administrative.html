HR & administrative talent

HR generalists, recruiters, payroll specialists, and administrative professionals who understand the GovCon workforce.

What We Support

People operations built for government contracting

HR at a government contractor comes with its own requirements: recruiting cleared talent, tracking wage determinations on service contracts, maintaining affirmative action obligations, and managing benefits across multiple locations and contracts.

We place HR, payroll, and administrative professionals who understand those requirements and can support your people from offer letter to offboarding.

Roles we place

  • HR generalists & managers
  • Recruiters & talent acquisition
  • Payroll specialists
  • Benefits administrators
  • Facility security officers (FSOs)
  • Office & executive assistants
